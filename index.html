Interior design in Pune

Thoughtful Interiors, Built for Everyday Life

Design DNA is an interior design studio in Pune for homeowners and businesses looking for a clear, end-to-end process. We bring interior design, architectural planning and civil execution together, so the ideas agreed at the start can be carried through with care on site.

From residential interior design and modular kitchen planning to renovations, turnkey projects and 3D views, each space is shaped around how you live, work and move through it. Our portfolio reflects a practical approach to materials, layout, lighting and finishing details.

Do you provide interior design and execution in Pune?

Yes. Design DNA handles the design direction and coordinates execution so planning, materials and on-site work stay aligned.

Can you manage turnkey interior projects?

Yes. Turnkey projects bring design, sourcing, civil work, execution and final handover together through one coordinated studio process.

Do you provide 3D interior views and walkthroughs?

Yes. Detailed 3D views and walkthroughs help you understand the proposed space before execution begins.

Do you work on residential renovations and modular kitchens?

Yes. We plan residential renovations, kitchens and living spaces with practical layouts, material selections and a complete design direction.

Can you help with interiors for compact urban homes?

Yes. We use space planning, storage, lighting and considered furniture layouts to make compact homes feel functional and personal.
